What stood out most was how confident the models sounded while being wrong. The language model reported that the cup was empty without ever seeing it, and the vision model called a drawing compass a tape measure three times in a row. In Mission 3 I rewrote my maze prompt three times, and the robot still turned DOWN at the same corner because that was the first allowed option.

It made me think about where I want to spend effort in robotics. Writing a better prompt helped in Mission 1, but the parts that actually kept the robot safe were ordinary code: the capability card, the wall check and the validator that refused stale or unauthorized proposals. I am more interested now in that layer between a model and a machine. If robots are going to take instructions from people, or from a poster on a wall, someone has to decide what counts as permission and evidence, and that feels just as important as the model itself.
